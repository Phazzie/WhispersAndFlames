# Whispers and Flames ExecPlan Standard

An ExecPlan is the living implementation contract for a multi-hour feature. It must be understandable without chat history and must name the observable behavior, exact files, interfaces, commands, recovery path, and acceptance evidence.

Every task plan includes: Purpose / Big Picture, Progress, Surprises & Discoveries, Decision Log, Outcomes & Retrospective, Context and Orientation, Plan of Work, Concrete Steps, Validation and Acceptance, Idempotence and Recovery, Artifacts and Notes, and Interfaces and Dependencies.

Keep Progress as checkboxes. Update Progress, Surprises & Discoveries, Decision Log, and Outcomes & Retrospective at every meaningful stopping point. Resolve architecture choices inside the plan rather than leaving them to an implementer. Prefer observable milestones and exact repository-relative commands over broad task lists.
